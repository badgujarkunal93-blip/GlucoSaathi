## 2. Permitted Usage & Product Integration Terms
1. **Clinical & Research Reference**: The nutritional values (carbohydrates, available energy, protein, dietary fiber, fat) in GlucoSaathi's lookup table are derived from standard published nutritional values for non-commercial decision support and patient education.
2. **Usage Restriction Notice**: The complete raw PDF compendium contains proprietary copyright terms prohibiting wholesale electronic scraping or redistribution as an independent commercial database product.
3. **GlucoSaathi Architecture**: GlucoSaathi uses a curated, domain-specific subset of key Indian dishes (e.g. `data/indianFoods.json`) mapped to standard portion metrics (*phulka*, *katori*, *plate*) for patient carb estimation. Any future commercial expansion requires formal institutional licensing from ICMR-NIN.
